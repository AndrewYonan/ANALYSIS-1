Let $$S(N,m) := Nm + \sum_{k=0}^{N-1} k$$ Let $a$ be a positive integer. Determine the remainder when $$\left( \sum_{i=1}^a S(a,i) - a \right)$$ is divided by $6$. \\ \\ 
$$\sum_{i=1}^a S(a,i) - a$$
$$ = \sum_{i=1}^a \left(ai + \sum_{k=0}^{a-1} k \right)- a$$
$$ = \frac{a^2(a+1)}{2} + \frac{a^2(a-1)}{2} - a$$
$$ = a^3 - a$$
$$ = (a-1)a(a+1)$$
Note that this is the product of $3$ consecutive integers, which is always divisible by $6$. Hence the remainder when the expression is divided by $6$ is $\boxed{0}$
